# Claraverse portfolio folder

The folder is the selected portfolio style and is the default on the home page.
The comparison switcher has been removed. Open `http://localhost:3000/#portfolio`.

Six original concept projects are included: form, ritual, dwelling, gather,
signal and terrain. Each has a distinct storefront visual, sector, summary,
operational scope and working `/work/<slug>` concept-study page. The work index
and detail pages share the same original visual assets.

The native SVG folder has a continuous tabbed outline, matte paper texture,
fold seams, a recessed interior, layered paper edges and contact shadows. A
slow light accent follows the actual outline. Its front pocket opens and closes;
selecting a project opens the folder and brings its file to the front.

Browse using the file cards, six thumbnails, previous/next buttons, or the
thumbnail group's Left/Right, Home and End keys. The chosen project's details
and file count update together. Mobile uses a bounded fan and a 3-by-2 grid.
Reduced motion disables the entrance, paper movement and animated edge.

Projects remain labeled as concept work. No client commissions or results are
claimed. Replace the shared data and photography with approved client work later.

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a single photographic triptych texture sheet for three original ecommerce concept case studies on a premium agency website. Create one seamless horizontal 3:1 image, ideally 3072x1024, divided into THREE EXACTLY EQUAL VERTICAL PANELS, each panel a complete portrait composition, no borders, no gutters, no text anywhere. LEFT THIRD: avant-garde fashion editorial still life, sculptural black folded garment and fluid cream fabric draped on a pale stone plinth, one subtle chrome accent, warm off-white background, strong tasteful sunlight and tactile textile. CENTER THIRD: premium beauty product still life, two elegant unbranded amber glass serum bottles and one sculptural pale cream skincare jar standing on warm terracotta stone, soft peach and warm sand background, dramatic long afternoon shadows and translucent glass highlights. RIGHT THIRD: architectural home and living editorial photograph, an organic moss-green lounge chair with pale oak side table and one small ceramic object, warm beige sculpted interior, sunlight entering at an angle, calm minimal composition. Cohesive luxury art direction across all three equal panels, physically convincing materials, high-end editorial photography, generous negative space in upper area for separately coded UI, restrained rich contrast. No people, no type, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no interface, no browser frames. Each subject is centered within its own third. The three panels must have completely separate backdrops and must not cross panel boundaries.
